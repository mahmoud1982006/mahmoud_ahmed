**Scenario:** Ransomware attack on a U.S. health care clinic

---

## Description

A small U.S. health care clinic experienced a ransomware attack after an employee opened a phishing email and downloaded a malicious attachment. The attachment installed ransomware on the employee's computer, which then encrypted critical company files, including patient medical records. As a result, employees lost access to the files and software needed to perform their jobs, and business operations were forced to shut down. A ransom note was displayed on affected computers, demanding payment in exchange for the decryption key.

## Additional Notes

Employees should receive regular security awareness training on how to recognize phishing emails and avoid downloading suspicious attachments, since the initial access vector in this incident was human error rather than a technical vulnerability. Reinforcing this first line of defense could have prevented the ransomware from ever reaching the network.
